Paper Scrubber

Take the student's name out of a paper before you paste it into an AI.
A free tool from KVEC
Kentucky Valley Educational Cooperative
✓ Papers stay on your computer ✓ Works on a Chromebook ✓ Nothing to install ✓ No sign-in, no cost

What it does

It finds names, addresses, phone numbers, birthdays and other personal details in a student's paper and swaps each one for a tag like [NAME 1]. Then you can ask ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude for help without giving away who wrote it.

How to use it

beadbeed3000.github.io/paper-scrubber
  1. Open the link in Chrome or Edge. The first time, click ▶ Try an example first and give it a minute to set up.
  2. Paste a paper, or click Choose papers or photos… (Word, PDF or a photo of a typed page). Then click Scrub this paper.
  3. Read it over. If it covered up something it shouldn't have, like a book character, click the tag to put the word back.
  4. Click 📋 Copy scrubbed text and paste it into the AI.
  5. Want the names back in the AI's answer? Paste the answer into the box that says Got the AI's reply?

Five ways teachers use it

Feedback on an essayAsk the AI what's working and what to fix next, then put it in your own words for the student.
Check against your rubricPaste your rubric with the paper and ask where it lands on each row.
A whole class setAdd every paper at once. Ask the AI what mistakes show up the most, so you know what to reteach.
Share a good exampleShow a strong paper to your PLC, another teacher or the next class without the student's name on it.
Help with a note homeScrub a draft email or write-up first, then ask the AI to make it clearer or kinder.

Three rules

  • Read every result before it goes to an AI. It catches most details, not all of them.
  • Never paste the original paper into an AI.
  • The AI gives ideas. You still do the grading.
