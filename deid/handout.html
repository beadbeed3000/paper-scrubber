De-Identifier

Take the student out of the record — before the record goes to an AI.
KVEC staff tool
Kentucky Valley Educational Cooperative
✓ Nothing leaves your laptop ✓ Works with no internet ✓ No sign-in, no cost ✓ No settings to fiddle with

What it does

Drop in an IEP or eval report — Word file, PDF, or a scan — and it hands back the same document with the student removed: names, ID numbers, birthdays, dates, school, address, family details, health details, even hobbies and interests that could give a kid away in a small school. Everything happens automatically. The goals, services, and data you need to review stay readable.

Set it up (once, takes five minutes)

github.com/beadbeed3000/paper-scrubber/releases/latest

On a Mac

  1. Download the file ending in .dmg, open it, drag De-Identifier into Applications.
  2. First time only: right-click the app → Open → Open. (It's ours, not from the App Store, so the Mac asks once.)

⊞ On Windows

  1. Download De-Identifier-Setup (.exe) and run it. No admin password needed.
  2. If Windows says "protected your PC": click More info → Run anyway. (Same reason — it's ours, once.)

Every day

  1. Drop the records in — one or a whole stack. Scanning a pile? Click 📠 Watch a scans folder once and every new scan handles itself.
  2. Let it work. A long IEP takes a few minutes. Start it, get your coffee.
  3. Give the result a one-minute read. The tool finds; you're the last set of eyes. Click anything it got wrong to fix just that word.
  4. Copy it into ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini and do your review. The AI sees [NAME 1] — never the student. Paste the AI's reply back into the tool and the real names return, on your machine.
The privacy promise, in one sentence: the record never leaves your laptop, and the AI you paste into never receives a name, a number, or a detail that points at a child — that's been tested against a full sample IEP, checked line by line, with a machine test that blocks any release that would leak.
The one rule: no software catches 100% — anyone who promises that is selling something. That's why step 3 exists. The tool does the work; a person reads last. One minute, every time.
