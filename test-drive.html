What it does

It takes the student's name and other identifying details out of an IEP or evaluation, so you can use an AI to help with the review. It runs on your computer. The record is never uploaded.

Get started (once)

beadbeed3000.github.io/paper-scrubber/deid
  1. On good Wi-Fi, open the link above in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Click ▶ Try an example first. The first time takes a few minutes while it downloads its AI. After that it works without internet.

Every record

  1. Click Choose records… (or paste the text), then De-identify this record. A long IEP takes about 2 minutes.
  2. Read the result. Look hardest at initials, dates and case numbers. Click any word to replace it or put it back.
  3. Click 📋 Copy de-identified text and paste it into the AI.
  4. To get the real names back, paste the AI's answer into the box that says Got the AI's reply?

Three rules

  • Try the example before your first real record.
  • Read every result before it goes to an AI. The tool catches most details, not all of them.
  • Never paste the original record into an AI.

Tell Alex what you find

That is the point of the test drive. Send anything it left in, anything it took out that you needed, and anything confusing or slow.

Email alex@theholler.org. Describe the problem in a sentence, like "it left the case number in the header." Never send the record itself.
