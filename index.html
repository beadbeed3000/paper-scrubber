Word .docx comes back as Word, with your formatting intact. .pdf and plain .txt come back as text. Selecting a whole class set at once is the fast way — click one file, then press Ctrl+A.

What language are the papers in?

The first scrub downloads the scrubber and keeps it on this computer. After that it runs with the Wi-Fi off — papers are never uploaded anywhere.

They arrive as one .zip in your Downloads folder — double-click it to open the papers inside. Word files stay Word files; PDFs come back as text.

Something highlighted that shouldn't be? Click it — or Tab to it and press Enter — to put the real word back. Handy for book characters like Atticus Finch. Underlined words are ones you kept.

Give it a quick read before you share it. No tool catches everything — you're still the last set of eyes.
