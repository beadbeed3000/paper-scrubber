Paper Scrubber — the one-page guide

Take the student out of the paper — before the paper goes to an AI.

A free tool from KVEC

AI tools are great at giving feedback on writing — and terrible at forgetting what you paste into them. Paper Scrubber swaps every name, address, and birthday for a tag like [NAME 1] right on your computer, so the robot helps with the essay without ever learning who wrote it.

← Back to Paper Scrubber
  1. 1

    Open it. Go to beadbeed3000.github.io/paper-scrubber — or point your phone's camera at the square. It runs on school laptops, Chromebooks, and phones, with nothing to install and no account to make.

  2. 2

    Feed it papers. Paste one, or drop Word, PDF, or photo files anywhere on the page — a whole class set at once is the fast way. On a phone, tap 📷 Take a photo and shoot the page straight on.

  3. 3

    Let it scrub. The first scrub downloads the scrubber once (give it a minute); after that it works with the Wi-Fi off. Every personal detail becomes a tag, and the same kid gets the same tag in every paper of the set.

  4. 4

    Be the last set of eyes. Skim the highlights. Book characters get flagged too — the scrubber has no idea Atticus Finch isn't in your third period — so click any highlight to put that word back. No tool catches everything; you're the teacher for a reason.

  5. 5

    Send it to the robot. Copy the scrubbed text into ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini and ask for feedback, a rubric check, or revision ideas. Word files can also come back as real Word files with formatting intact.

  6. 6

    Put the names back. When the reply says "Nice hook, [NAME 1]!", paste it into the box at the bottom of the review screen and the real names return — on your device, like everything else.

Good to know:
  • Nothing leaves the machine. Don't take our word for it — after your first scrub, turn the Wi-Fi off and keep scrubbing.
  • Photos and scans work when the paper is typed. Handwriting comes back as alphabet soup, and the app will say so.
  • Teaching Spanish? Pick "Spanish, French & more" before you scrub and the accents come out right.
